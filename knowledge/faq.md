# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Department Information

## Department & Specialty Information
- **Cardiology**: Diagnoses and manages heart and vascular conditions. Requires prior health history review.
- **Dermatology**: Specializes in skin, hair, and nail health. Routine skin checkups take 30 minutes.
- **Pediatrics**: Comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents up to age 18.
- **General Practice / Family Medicine**: Primary care for general health checkups, preventive health, and routine referrals.

## Preparation Instructions
- Bring a list of current prescription medications to your appointment.
- Fasting is required 8 hours prior to routine morning blood work if requested by your doctor.
- Wear comfortable clothing for physical examinations or physical therapy sessions.

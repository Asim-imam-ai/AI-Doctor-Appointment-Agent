# Clinic Appointment Policy

## General Information
- The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM EST.
- Emergency services are not provided directly at our outpatient clinics. In an emergency, please dial 911 or visit the nearest emergency room.

## Booking Policy
- Patients may book appointments online up to 30 days in advance.
- Standard consultation duration is 30 minutes.
- New patients must arrive 15 minutes prior to their scheduled time with a valid photo ID.

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
- Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled start time without any penalty.
- Late cancellations (less than 24 hours notice) or missed appointments (no-shows) may incur a $50 administrative fee.
- To cancel or reschedule, patients may use the online agent portal or contact clinic support directly.
